# Threat Intelligence

This repository section is dedicated to collecting, analyzing, and applying cyber threat intelligence to proactively defend against modern adversaries.

### Key Areas of Study:
* **Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)** — Tactical, operational, and strategic intelligence workflows.
* **Indicators of Compromise (IoCs)** — Tracking and managing hashes, IP addresses, and malicious domains.
* **Threat Frameworks** — Utilizing the MITRE ATT&CK framework and Cyber Kill Chain to map adversary behavior.
